Bing Beats · Apr 2026

An app for discovering music by place and time. Pick a country and a year from 1950 to today, and it lists the tracks that defined that place and moment, with 30-second previews and links to the full songs on Spotify. A chat assistant explains the local scene and can suggest playlists. It runs on iOS, Android, and the web; a small Express backend keeps API keys off the client and combines Spotify, iTunes, Gemini, and Mapbox.

Built with: TypeScript, Expo / React Native, Node.js, Express

MegaGem Live · Feb 2026

A digital version of Jane Street's auction board game MegaGems: one human against two AI bidders. At the start of each round, half the gems are secretly dealt out as private information, and winning an auction forces you to reveal one of them, so every bid is a trade between value and information. A Flask and Socket.IO backend runs the real-time bidding; the game is played in a first-person 3D auction room built with Three.js (with a flat 2D mode), and Gemini powers an in-game coach and a tutorial.

Built with: Python, Flask, Socket.IO, JavaScript, Three.js, Gemini API

Global Asset Management Distribution · Jan – May 2025

Independent research on where the world's top asset management companies are located. I aggregated and cross-checked market data to study how being close to a major financial center relates to a firm's long-term success, and published the results as an interactive story map.
